# Data licenses

The tiles published here are built from US government public data. (The app also looks up FAA aeronautical
studies live from the FAA's OE/AAA site; nothing from that is published or stored here.)

| Files | Source | License |
|---|---|---|
| `tile_*.sqlz` (FCC data plus the `obstacle` table), `index.sqlz` | FCC ASR, ULS and broadcast databases, and the FAA Digital Obstacle File | US government public data; no restrictions. FCC data is as filed and may be out of date. The FAA says the DOF covers only objects that affect aeronautical charting, not every obstruction, and that "unverified" records have unverified positions and heights. |
| `*.py`, workflow | this repo | (add a license before accepting outside contributions) |

Data is as filed with the agencies and may be out of date or inaccurate. Not affiliated with the FCC or FAA.
